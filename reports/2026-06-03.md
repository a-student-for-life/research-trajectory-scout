**Abstract:**

Multivariate time series classification (MTSC) is pivotal in high-stakes domains, such as clinical diagnosis and industrial fault detection, where safe deployment necessitates transparent decision-making. However, isolating the temporal segments that drive model predictions is challenging because discriminative signals in real-world time series are typically sparse, heterogeneous, and heavily obscured by background noise. This paper, therefore, proposes AnchorMoE, an interpretable-by-construction classification framework.
